---
name: Achievement rules
description: Product rules for XQution account achievements and owner controls.
---

The first 100 accounts by creation time qualify as Veterans; the Veteran achievement becomes unavailable after that cohort. Owner achievement toggles hide or show achievements globally but preserve each user's earned progress.

**Why:** The product defines Veteran as a first-100-account achievement, while deleting progress when an owner temporarily disables an achievement would be surprising.

**How to apply:** Keep these rules consistent when changing achievement definitions, owner controls, or account progress calculations.
