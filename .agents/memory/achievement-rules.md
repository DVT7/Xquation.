---
name: Achievement rules
description: Product rules for XQution account achievements and owner controls.
---

The first ten accounts by creation time qualify as first-site veterans. Owner achievement toggles hide or show achievements globally but preserve each user's earned progress.

**Why:** The product request did not specify a veteran cutoff, and deleting progress when an owner temporarily disables an achievement would be surprising.

**How to apply:** Keep these rules consistent when changing achievement definitions, owner controls, or account progress calculations.
